Guest suite

Unit 1

A peaceful, private guest suite offering comfortable self-catering accommodation in Bloemfontein. Unit 1 features two bedrooms, one bathroom and sleeps up to four guests, making it well suited to families, couples travelling together, business travellers and guests visiting Bloemfontein for school or sporting events.

The house has everything you need for a comfortable stay, including a fully equipped kitchen, comfortable living area, private outdoor space and braai facilities. An indoor fireplace adds a cosy touch for cooler evenings, while the peaceful surroundings provide a relaxing place to unwind after a busy day.

Unit 1 is also pet-friendly, so you can bring your four-legged companion along. Wildlife regularly visits the property, giving guests the opportunity to enjoy a little nature during their stay, while still being only 2 km from the nearest shops and close to the convenience of Bloemfontein.

Whether you are staying for a night on your way through the city, attending an event, visiting for school sport or spending a few days in Bloemfontein, Unit 1 offers a comfortable home away from home.
