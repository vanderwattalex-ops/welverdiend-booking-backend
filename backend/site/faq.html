Booking, payment and your stay

Can I cancel, and do I get my money back?

Cancel 5 days or more before check-in and you get a full refund. Less than 5 days before check-in, or if you don't arrive, there's no refund. You can also move your dates free of charge if the new dates are open.

How do I pay?

After we confirm your dates, a 50% deposit by EFT within 24 hours secures your stay. The balance is due before check-in, and we send you a reminder 2 days before.

Is there a minimum stay?

No. One night is fine, any time.

Do you offer a discount for longer stays?

Yes, when you book direct: 3+ nights 3% off, 5+ nights 7% off, 7+ nights 10% off and 28+ nights 20% off the nightly rate. It's applied automatically on the booking page. If a special is running, you get whichever saves you more.

How does check-in work?

Self check-in from 14:00 with a lockbox, so you can arrive whenever suits you, with no charge for arriving late. We send check-in instructions, the gate details and a pin drop before your stay. Check-out is by 10:00; leave the keys in the lockbox.

Are bed linen and towels provided?

Yes: bed linen, towels, soap, shampoo, toilet paper, a hairdryer, and tea, coffee, sugar and milk. The kitchen has a stove and oven, microwave, fridge-freezer, dishwasher, kettle, toaster and coffee maker.

What size are the beds?

In both units, bedroom 1 has a double bed and bedroom 2 has two single beds.

Are children welcome?

Yes, all ages, and babies under 2 don't count towards the 4 guests. A baby bath is available on request. Please supervise children around the pool, the swimming dam, the animals and the fireplace.

Is there a pool?

Yes. A shared farm-style pool with a sun deck, open all year, behind a lockable gate. Pets aren't allowed in the pool area.

How far are you from the N1, hospitals and schools?

The N1 on-ramp is 3.6 km (4 minutes) away and the nearest supermarket 2.5 km. Mediclinic Bloemfontein, Universitas Hospital, Grey College and the University of the Free State are all 7–8 km (11–12 minutes), Toyota Stadium is 9 km and Loch Logan Waterfront 9.4 km. See all distances.

Can I bring a caravan, boat or trailer?

Yes. Each unit has 2 covered parking bays, and there's room for bigger vehicles.

Can I smoke?

Outside only. Quiet hours are 22:00 to 06:00, and there are no parties, events or day visitors.
