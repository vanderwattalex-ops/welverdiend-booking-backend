Why it suits a sport or match weekend

  • A whole house for the family. Each unit has two bedrooms (one double bed, one with two single beds), a lounge and a fully equipped kitchen. Sleeps up to four.
  • Two units on one property. Coming with another family? Book both units and stay on the same property.
  • Back late after a night match? Check-in is from 14:00 with self check-in by lockbox, so you can arrive whenever suits you.
  • Braai after the game. Each unit has a covered braai, and there's a shared farm-style swimming pool, open all year.
  • Bring the dog. Pets stay free. See our pet policy.
  • Covered parking inside the fenced property, 2 bays per unit.
